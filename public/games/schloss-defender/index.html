GAME OVERVIEW

Welcome, Defender! Schloss Defender is an intense arcade action-defense game. Your mission is to protect the Castle Wall on the left from endless hordes of German tourist-themed invaders and their elite allies.

Core Rules & HUD

  • Castle Gate HP: Starts at 100 HP. When mobs reach the gate (x = 90 limit), they breach it and deal damage before dying. If HP hits 0, the castle falls!
  • Score & Combo: Defeating mobs builds up your Combo Multiplier. Higher combo = exponentially more points!
  • Combo Break: Taking any damage resets your combo to 1 and deducts -200 points from your score.
  • Stuns & IFrames: After taking damage, you gain 0.75s of damage immunity (iframes). However, certain wizard or magic attacks can stun you.

HERO ABILITIES

Harness your weapon styles and active abilities to secure the perimeter. Practice makes perfect.

Sword Slash Key: J

Swings in a 75px radius. Deals 100 base damage + 6.25 per Berserk stack. Cooldown: 0.5s (down to 0.25s at max Berserk). Hitting adds Berserk stacks and shatters frozen targets.

Frost Arrow Key: L

Shoots a swift arrow dealing 50 damage. Charges up frost energy as it travels. Applies 1 to 3 Ice Slow Stacks based on distance. If fully charged, it freezes the target.

Forcefield Shield Key: K

Creates a defensive shield around you for 4 seconds. Blocks enemy projectiles and knocks back enemies, dealing 25 contact damage. Cooldown: 10s. Knockbacks extend duration!

Supernova Fire Key: I

Ignites the entire battlefield, dealing 100 damage to all enemies and melting armor (allowing slashes to bypass guard shield blocks). Cooldown: 20s.

ADVANCED FORMULAS

For the hardcore strategists. Here are the internal math and multipliers governing the game loop.

Berserk Mode Scaling

Every slash hit adds 1 Berserk stack (caps at 8). Stacks scale your power:
• Slash Damage: 100 + (stacks × 6.25) HP (up to 150 damage max).
• Slash Cooldown: 30 - (stacks × 1.875) frames (down to 15 frames max / 0.25s).
• Radius & Wave: Adds 3.125px radius per stack. At 8 stacks, slashes emit a cyan shockwave dealing 75 damage in an extra 10px radius.

Freeze Slow System

Bow hits apply Ice slow stacks which compound speed reduction:
• 1 stack: 50% speed multiplier reduction.
• 2 stacks (Freeze Blast): Triggers an explosion in a 150px radius applying 2 stacks and poison (if arrow was poisoned) to nearby enemies.
• 3 stacks (Freeze Nuke): Triggers a massive explosion in a 200px radius dealing 50 damage and applying 3 stacks to nearby enemies, spawning 16 radial freeze arrows.
